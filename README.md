## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes food delivery performance to identify **key factors causing delays** and provide **data-driven recommendations** for improving operational efficiency.

The analysis focuses on:
- 🌦️ Weather Conditions  
- 🚦 Road Traffic Density  
- 🏙️ City Type (Urban vs Metropolitan)  

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Delivery delays directly impact **customer satisfaction and business revenue**.  

This project aims to answer:
- What factors contribute most to delivery delays?  
- How do traffic and weather affect delivery time?  
- Are there differences between urban and metro cities?

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 🚦 **High traffic density** leads to the longest delivery delays  
- 🌧️ **Adverse weather conditions** significantly increase delivery time  
- 🏙️ **Metropolitan areas** show more variability compared to urban areas  
- 🎯 Filtering (slicers) helps identify patterns across different scenarios  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- ✅ Optimize delivery routes during peak traffic hours  
- ✅ Introduce **dynamic delivery time estimation** based on weather & traffic  
- ✅ Allocate more delivery agents in high-demand zones  
- ✅ Use predictive analytics for delay forecasting
